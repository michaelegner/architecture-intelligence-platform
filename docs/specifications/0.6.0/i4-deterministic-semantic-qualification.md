## 1. Purpose and exit result

I4 independently qualifies the *implemented* question, **“Where is this direct HTTP dependency positively established, and how do the supported answers differ between selected evidenced caller localities?”** It establishes that AIP's answer follows the frozen evidence and locality rules, survives clean-state replay, agrees across the service, REST and negotiated MCP, and does not weaken v0.5 behaviour.

I4 adds **qualification evidence**, not architecture semantics or another public feature. The result is a reviewed I4 qualification record and I5 handoff naming an exact tested SHA, executable expected truth, two independent clean runs, failures/skips, semantic differences, compatibility/security findings and measured cost. The result is *I4 qualified for handoff*, not `RELEASE_READY` and not a substitute for the exact I6 final-candidate rerun.

**Blocking invariants:** no false supported caller locality, declaration promoted to a local call without admitted observation, local absence inferred from missing evidence, Workload identity inferred from name/co-location or `DEPLOYED_AS` alone, target locality or causal path inferred from caller-local edges, undisclosed source/capture selection, cross-snapshot evidence substitution, incorrect v1/v2 coexistence, or drift between service/REST/MCP meaning. Returning everything `UNRESOLVED` is not a passing evaluation.

## 2. Scope and boundaries

**In scope:** independent positive/negative truth tables and traceability; query/evidence-mode answers and refusals; C1 → C2 capture churn and selected-source applicability; v1/v2 ingestion and no-double-counting; same-snapshot and no-v2 pins; bounded pages, caps and deterministic comparisons; v0.5.0/v0.5.1 compatibility; security/read-only regression; a measured Pod-churn/cardinality budget and impact assessment; repeatable evaluation and a handoff.

**Outside I4:** collecting or claiming the independently captured *actual* two-Workload rollout (I5); modifying frozen Quarkus/Airflow upstream dossiers (I5); the real-system qualification or a user demo/pilot (I5); flipping `telemetry.scoped-evidence.enabled`, re-freezing the pinned release golden path at four tools, version/tag/image publication and the technical `GO` decision (I6); new region/tenant/service-version/messaging-locality support, historical snapshot browsing, target placement, Intent, and ADR 0012 retention/compaction. Existing finding #323 is tracked, not silently “fixed” by I4.

**Mandatory I4-P3 bridge vector (a gap in I3, not an amendment to its oracle):** Independently author and freeze a same-day C1 → C2 rollout input **before** running AIP. C1 captures caller Pod **P1**, its UID and owner chain, and its own v2 CALLS; after authoritative replacement, C2 captures a *different* Pod **P3** under the same named Deployment and supplies P3's own independently attributable v2 CALLS. Author two variants: **(a)** the Deployment/Workload UID is unchanged across C1/C2; **(b)** the same name represents a *new* captured Workload UID/incarnation. In **each** C2 variant, P1's retained v2 record stays Pod-scoped but its old Workload-local assessment is `UNRESOLVED` when C2 no longer contains its UID/owner chain; no P1 evidence can be reassigned to P3 or used to qualify the current Workload merely because its name or even its Workload UID matches. P3's positive assessment and evidence refs must derive **only from P3's own v2 and admissible C2 capture**. Verify absence of P1→P3 evidence leakage in candidate/pair results, provider/Operation grouping, selected-locality comparison and scoped drill-down. Include C1's prior positive result against its then-current snapshot, but do not claim a historical C1 query is still available after C2 replaces it. Explicitly pin expected positive, unresolved and forbidden facts, capture revisions, UTC-day and snapshot semantics; verify both variants against real write paths and the I4 two-run harness. The independently recorded I2.6a rehearsal remains unchanged and does not itself cover P3.

**Coverage rule:** each parent §20 scenario must have an independently grounded expected outcome and a specific executable assertion or a documented `NOT_COVERED` blocker. Counting a test merely because its name resembles the scenario is insufficient. Any genuine ambiguity is resolved by a reviewed specification amendment *before* changing a frozen expectation.

## 4. Two clean-state execution and deterministic comparison

1. Select and record **one full 40-hex `I4_CANDIDATE_SHA`** from an immutable commit, including all I4 qualification code and the frozen expectation register. **Pass this exact value explicitly** to the oracle harness, both clean-run workers, existing/new evaluators, benchmarks and the result ledger; each component SHALL assert that its checkout `HEAD`, configured producer/build revision and recorded candidate identity agree with it before qualifying output. In particular, `benchmarks/scoped_churn_cost.py` and `benchmarks/locality_cost.py` currently record ambient `git rev-parse HEAD`; I4 must add an explicit candidate parameter/validated wrapper rather than treating that ambient value as proof. Record fixture digests, published-schema digests, configuration (including the scoped-evidence flag), importer/source revisions, Neo4j/runtime versions and command. Read CI statuses for **this exact SHA** through `/repos/<owner>/<repo>/commits/<sha>/check-runs` (not just `gh pr checks`), including run/check IDs, status and conclusion. No floating `main`, placeholder build revision, mismatched SHA or silently changed worktree qualifies.
2. Execute the **entire mandatory scenario set twice**, runs **A** and **B**, in **separate Python/pytest processes**, each with a **fresh disposable Neo4j container and independent fixture/source state**. The existing I3 harness caches (`tests/integration/locality_oracle/world.py::_REPLAYED` and `tests/integration/test_locality_rehearsal_replay.py::_RESULTS`) may optimize *within* one run but MUST NOT bridge A and B. Record distinct process/container identities, clean-state reset proof, exact SHA and source/fixture digests for each run; a second loop over cases in the same pytest process does **not** count as B. Recreate state per scenario or declared scenario sequence. For the staged canary, replay **C1 then C2 within each run** (including both P3 variants) to test the transition; reset between A and B. Do not conflate different C1 and C2 states with two identical runs.
3. Each run consumes the same independently frozen inputs and request contexts. Compare against frozen expected/forbidden facts using the normative I1/I2/I3 matching rules **only for oracle conformance** (including the I3 symbols). Capture the actual complete semantic envelopes (not only `pytest` exit codes), query and evidence responses, identifiers, ordered candidates/pairs, limitations, source/capture revisions and `SnapshotRef`.
4. **No semantic normalization for A versus B.** Compare **raw canonical JSON bytes** for the same case/state/transport after deterministic canonical serialization; hash the bytes and also retain them or reproducible artifact pointers. **No** placeholder substitution, generated-ID remapping, field removal or masking is allowed in the A/B comparison: `snapshot_id`, assessment/assertion IDs, source/capture IDs, Workload UIDs, provenance, limitations and version/build identity remain in the comparison. For **service versus REST versus MCP** within a run, remove **only** the outer HTTP/MCP transport wrapper and compare the *exact* service semantic JSON bytes; do not alter the payload. I3 matrix symbol binding and set matching apply **only when comparing each output to its frozen oracle**, never to A/B or adapter parity. If a fixture emits nondeterministic fields, fix its fixture/authoring before a new run; do not introduce post-hoc normalization. A stable refusal must compare identically as well.
5. A requested `snapshot_id` and any continuation cursor must remain bound to its original snapshot across calls. Capture replacement or source revision change must change/refuse results where the frozen rules require it. Reordered imports, returned row order, candidate order and independently permitted permutations must not change semantic identity or output.
6. Persist a machine-readable run ledger per case/state: explicit `I4_CANDIDATE_SHA` and verified `HEAD`/producer/build revision; distinct run A/B process and fresh-Neo4j container identifiers; input/oracle/schema/source/config digests; captured source/state; **raw canonical semantic-byte digest per surface**; direct oracle match result; exact-byte A/B and cross-surface comparator results; omitted/skipped cases; and mismatch evidence. Include CI check-run IDs and conclusions retrieved for that SHA. Record reproducible raw result artifacts or mismatch excerpts sufficient for independent inspection. Do not hand-edit results or normalize away failures.

**Acceptance:** zero A/B canonical-byte differences, zero mismatch against supported expected/forbidden facts, zero cross-surface semantic-byte differences for equivalent requests, and zero falsely positive locality or negative-dependency claims. Unsupported or incomplete evidence must lead to the **specified** abstention, never a broad “pass by omission.” An unexecuted required case, unexplained nondeterminism, or a fixture changed after seeing AIP's output is a blocking finding.

**Candidate identity across increments:** I4 qualifies its pinned `I4_CANDIDATE_SHA`. If I5/I6 changes code, schema, fixtures, default configuration or release packaging, I6 must repeat the required qualification on the eventual **exact final `CANDIDATE_SHA`**; an earlier I4 SHA does not automatically qualify that later candidate. This makes the I3 handoff's “exact final candidate” requirement enforceable without claiming a future I6 SHA already exists.

## 5. Public surface, compatibility and security gates

- **Four tools, two modes and two new POST routes.** Direct `ArchitectureIntelligenceService`, REST and *negotiated* MCP (including real-HTTP independent-client discovery/call/reconnect) must return exactly the same canonical `LocalityAnswer` bytes at the same snapshot, removing *only* the HTTP/MCP transport wrapper. Compare `query` and `evidence`, valid partial/refused results, cursor continuation, snapshot mismatch and unauthorized v2/capture refs. **Execute the frozen Q01–Q08 invalid-request inputs through the actual REST routes (HTTP 422) and negotiated MCP (`isError: true`)**, in addition to the existing model-level rejection tests; do not count similar adapter tests as execution of Q01–Q08. HTTP status and MCP wrapping are the only permitted transport differences for successful semantic answers.
- **Versioned contract.** Public `0.6` answer/request schemas and the fourth tool's advertised `inputSchema.request` / `outputSchema` are byte/structurally pinned as already frozen. Existing `0.5` schemas, routes and three tools keep their meanings. No unreviewed field, fifth tool or generic graph traversal.
- **Legacy regressions.** Run the existing deterministic relation and architecture-answer evaluators, v0.5.1 Quarkus replay/demo pins, ingestion and correlation, source-lifecycle/import, Path A/B/C deployment reconciliation, Pub/Sub/AsyncAPI and qualification tests. Verify no-v2 snapshot ID compatibility, no v1-count inflation, shared canonical snapshot with a Path B mapping artifact, and no synthetic location inferred from a Service deployment.
- **Isolation and safety.** Public reads must not write to the graph. Legacy `get_evidence`, `/api/evidence/*` and NL must not gain scoped-v2 access, and the new resolver must not disclose another caller's/Operation's evidence or an unauthorized capture ref. Missing and unauthorized refs remain indistinguishable as `NOT_FOUND`. Check sanitized fields, bounded refs/pages, read-only annotations, protocol/guard behaviour, dependency/security scans and published-schema validation.
- **Known security debt.** #323's independently known legacy NL → Kubernetes Evidence exposure remains an open finding with an owner/disposition; no result may imply I4 corrected it. A new exposure or a regression attributable to locality is an I4 blocker.

Use existing suites as executable primitives rather than inventing an independent second projection. In particular, `tests/integration/test_locality_oracle.py`, `test_locality_surface_parity.py`, `test_mcp_independent_client_golden_path.py`, the I1/I2 conformance suites and the frozen-schema tests are required inputs. The I4 comparison *records their actual semantic answers and fixture identity*, not merely aggregate green test counts.

## 6. Pod churn, retained state and observed cost

I4 SHALL extend/re-run the existing `benchmarks/scoped_churn_cost.py` and `benchmarks/locality_cost.py` measurements against **the same explicitly passed and verified `I4_CANDIDATE_SHA`** (§4). Their I2/I3 recordings are historical baselines, not an SLO or an I4 run. The I2 benchmark **already measures 0/100/1,000 distinct same-day Pod UIDs under one fixed Workload**; I4's genuinely new work is to measure **the same graph before and after an authoritative C1 → C2 capture removes P1 and introduces P3**, with both unchanged-UID and new-incarnation Workload variants (§3), and to quantify the resulting `UNRESOLVED` and capture-selection rates. Rerun/compare the existing 0/100/1,000-Pod anchors and the I3 **2,000 admitted-pair** covering-source bound so source count and *actual* candidate × source work are not conflated. Require explicit candidate-SHA acceptance in both benchmarks or a wrapper that passes and validates the SHA inside each process before reporting a measurement.

Record, by point and selected state: accepted/persisted v1/v2 records, per-POST ingestion cost, scoped and total retained nodes/relationships or evidence bytes, snapshot/fingerprint cost, fenced candidate read, owner read, projection/serialization, evidence lookup, complete cursor-walk counts, capture-selection/`UNRESOLVED`/refusal rates, and retries. Disclose the real-import versus disclosed X25 clone distinction. Record host/runtime versions, the explicitly passed and verified benchmark/candidate SHA, repetitions, C1/C2 state and source digests, separate process/container identity, and any measurements not obtained.

**Growth/bound budget:** D4's fixed per-page bounds (2,000 pairs, 500 candidates max before `k` reduction, 50 Workloads, 200 memberships, 20 evidence refs) remain unchanged. Before calling the measurements “acceptable,” the owner must record a reviewed **capacity/operational disposition** for Pod-churn growth, retained-state cost and capture overhead, including observed ratios and the limits of one host/run. This specification sets **no invented latency, memory, retention or product SLO**. A steep growth curve or impractical capture requirement is reported as a product-impact finding, not hidden by truncation, caching, coarsening or deleting scoped history. ADR 0012 remains proposed.

## 7. Findings, result and I5 handoff

Classify each tested case in an **I4-only ledger namespace**: `I4_CORRECT`, `I4_MISSING_SUPPORTED`, `I4_UNSUPPORTED_EXPECTED`, `I4_UNRESOLVED_EXPECTED`, `I4_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_EXPECTED`, or `I4_SEMANTIC_DEFECT`, with original evidence, expected scope and the payload's **separately recorded** original disposition/limitation code. The `I4_…_EXPECTED` classifications mean a *correctly expected* negative/abstaining result, never a blanket pass for every `UNRESOLVED` payload. This is qualification-ledger vocabulary **only** and does not change the public response enums or the I2 disposition values. Separately record harness/environment failures, skipped cases, known debt, unsupported scope and product/performance findings. Do not mark a missing supported locality as successful solely because an abstention is safe.

Any material defect stops I4 qualification. Fix the semantic owner or an independently established test-harness error, review any necessary contract amendment, and rerun **all affected and mandatory end-to-end gates at the new SHA**. Do not edit frozen I1/I2/I3 expected values to accommodate code. The I4 record must not claim `QUALIFIED` while a mandatory row is skipped or has an unexplained mismatch.

The I4 completion record SHALL contain the exact tested HEAD and CI/check-run IDs, two run IDs and per-case digests, the independently authored coverage/forbidden register, source/fixture pins, full mismatch/skip disposition, cross-surface equality results, v0.5 security/compatibility results, measured churn/read/retained-state cost and budget disposition, open finding owners and limits, and any post-I4 change requiring an I6 rerun.

The **I5 handoff** identifies the frozen contract, test vectors and I4-qualified SHA; the still-`NOT_RUN` real controlled two-Workload capture; the required clusterUid/CLIENT `k8s.cluster.uid`, Pod UID/owner chain, temporal and C1/C2 overlap pins; the non-inference/refusal controls; and what the later live capture must establish **independently**. The C1/C2 rehearsal is not presented as the I5 qualifying capture.
