# AIP × Glamorous Toolkit — v0.8 contract reference experiment

**Status:** lightweight **pre-v0.9** experiment; Draft 0.16, demonstration only; **I1 frozen** ([freeze record](i1-freeze/README.md)); GT implementation/evaluation pending  
**GT baseline:** official v1.1.657, bundled gt4llm v0.7.341  
**AIP source:** public read-only Sokol111 MCP `https://mcp.daip.io/mcp` (frozen pre-wave-1 C1 evidence)

**Sokol111 is a sample e-commerce codebase, not a production customer.** We do **not** need approval from its real maintainers or upstream Sokol111 code changes to demonstrate AIP's promise-inspection concepts. Fictional existing-service roles make explicit demo decisions labeled `DEMO_ASSUMED_CONFIRMED`. The proposed new consumer has **no seeded decision or Promise Contract**; a `DEMO_PROPOSED` consumer promise is possible only as an investigator-produced output. These are **not genuine owner-confirmed promises or AIP status enums**.

**Scenario:** A merchant wants to reuse its shop catalog to maintain product listings on an additional marketplace. Most requirements—product details, prices, availability, images, updates—are similar. **One small difference:** a disabled or deleted shop product must be withdrawn from the marketplace **within 15 minutes**. That is a hypothetical merchant requirement, **not** a current Sokol111 guarantee or real marketplace policy. Orders, checkout and fulfillment are excluded. The Marketplace Connector does not yet exist as an observed binding.

**Service-local Promise Contracts:** The [four service-local Promise Contracts and independent marketplace contract](demo-promises/README.md) describe **why** Catalog, Image and Product Query offer or accept capabilities, **what** each independently commits to, **to whom** and **under what conditions**. Product Query has separate fictional commitments: `PQ-ACCEPT-01` (Catalog event acceptance for the existing projection) and `PQ-READ-01` (read capability for separately provisioned tenant-scoped display clients, potentially including approved sales-channel integrations). The modeled marketplace reports completion/failure for accepted requests **without a numeric processing SLA**. **No acceptance or outward promise for the proposed Marketplace Connector is prepared in advance.** A **Promise Contract is an attributable offer or acceptance; its externally offered/accepted behavior is the promise.** In a real organization, the corresponding team would own and approve its Promise Contract; this reference overlay simulates that locality without misattribution.

**Central question:** Can an architect use the existing local offers and evidence to **derive a justified decision about whether the connector should accept and make any new promises**? The business deadline is the requirement—not an automatically accepted outward commitment. A valid outcome may be to defer or decline that promise.

**[GT tooling (I2)](gt/README.md):** neutral GT 1.1.657 views, the participant-corpus builder and an empty investigation page. The I2 done-when is assessed in I3.

**[Read the specification](specification.md)** — three increments: (1) freeze four existing-service Promise Contracts, one external-marketplace contract and evidence; (2) one promise-first GT/Lepiter investigation **that produces a consumer decision, not a seeded promise**; (3) fair comparison and findings. The marketplace's fictional accepted-withdrawal condition is separate from upstream change propagation and any connector responsibility. Incomplete AIP v0.8 qualification is **not a blocker**. GT may display the demo Promise Contracts separately from v0.8 qualified broker evidence; never present the Promise Contracts as native AIP-qualified behavioral Intent.

**Article lens:** Adapted from [Daniel Kocot, *Integration Beyond the Interface*](https://adorsys.com/en/techradar/integration-beyond-the-interface%20/): Why and for whom is each capability offered? What may the consumer rely on? Is use permitted? Who promises what independently? Can the commitments remain compatible as things change?

**Evaluation:** Compare source/Promise-Contract-only agent, GT without AIP, GT+AIP, and direct agent+AIP on identical inputs; use independent sessions/investigators or counterbalance order. **Primary insight test:** record the architect's prior belief, the discovered promise/evidence, and whether that changed or meaningfully strengthened the consumer decision—attributing AIP value only to its qualified contribution. Score correctly scoped promises, justified or declined new commitments, unsupported assertions, unexpected validated insights and maintenance cost separately from document discovery. **E1** is incremental AIP qualification; **E2** is fictional promise-navigation feasibility. Doctrine H1 (real decision value) / H2 (sustainable owner effort) still require real customers.

Historical [v0.4.2](../0.4.2/README.md) and [v0.5.1](../0.5.1/README.md) remain unchanged. No real owner approval, production marketplace connector, GT implementation, or measured product value is claimed.
