  `37496253900` (16:30:33Z), all `success`.

  (same parent `aa94eaf…`), with byte-identical fixture content. The candidate `e039a4c…` is a
  descendant of the squash commit, not of `c0fde5e`.
- This record, which names the commit, was written after readiness and publication. The readiness
  comment does not name it.

The frozen transcription is therefore identified by its content in `16049389440a…` (and, identically,

`16049389440a…`. The fixture bytes did not change between those commits, so the deviation is one of
ordering and recording, not of qualified content.
